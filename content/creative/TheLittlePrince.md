This document was created mapping the
cartesian coordinates to the polar
coordinates. We were visiting The Cube in
Orange County and I shot a 360 panorama of
Devin. So the x axis of the image represented
the azimuth and the y axes the elevation.
After that it was a simple matter of mapping
the cartesian coordinates to polar
coordinates.

Reach out to me if you want to learn how to
do it. Creatively it turned out to be an
interesting exercise, my version of the
__Asteroid B-612__ from the Little Prince,
complete with a space ship and some
baobab trees.
