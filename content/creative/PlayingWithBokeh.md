Bokeh (pronounced /bo'ka/) is the visual
quality of the out-of-focus areas of a
photographic image, especially as rendered
by a particular lens.

Most photo buffs including me primarily get
high quality Bokeh with good quality glass.
One quality of bokeh is the fact that out of
focus highlights usually are in the shape of
the aperture of a lens. Mirror lens typically
show this in the form of a donut bokeh.
In this image, I cut an opening in a piece of
cardboard in the form of a christmas tree
which is pasted in front of the lens. Now out
of focus highlights are rendered in the
shapes of the christmas tree.
