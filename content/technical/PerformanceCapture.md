This image was my first foray into motion
capture for Species in BOSS days. We all
looked so young. SIL motion capture rig was
all created internally at BOSS films. You can
see Richard Edlund on the bottom right.
This was really early days when we didn't
know what was noise and what was signal. In
the actual movie a lot of times the
performance got filtered out with the noise.

Oh well...

The next foray that I was going to have with
motion capture was with Polar Express and
Monster House. I wish I had some pix of the
polar express test. It was amazing to see that
the hardest working person on the set (~30-
40 people) was Tom Hanks and had great
attitude through out.


We looked into using active motion capture
for Auryn but that movie never got green lit.
