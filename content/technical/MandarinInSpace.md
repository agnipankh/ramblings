Even though I have supervised developing of
hundreds of apps, when boys were learning
Mandarin in kindergarten, I decided to write
this game myself to get them to practice their
Mandarin. It is essentially a flash card game
and actually can be extended to support any
flash card implementations.

The game was written in Unity 4 and I was
surprised how much work was generating the
art assets. I implemented Hermann
Ebbinghaus forgetting curve to ensure that
when kids got something correct the system
didn't repeat it immediately.

Apple removed the app from the app store
since l hadn't updated in a while. In the
meantime, enjoy this video... until I get the
time to update it.
